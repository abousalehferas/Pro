About Feras

Who am I?

A German teacher who understands the journey because I learned German as a foreign language myself.

My name is Feras. I am a passionate German teacher with over 5 years of teaching experience and more than 12 years of living, studying, and working in Germany.

Having learned German myself as a foreign language, I know the challenges firsthand. I know what it feels like not to understand a difficult grammar rule, and I know how to explain it in a way that finally makes sense.

I speak Arabic and English fluently. This means I can help you in the language you understand best, especially if Arabic or English is your mother tongue.

I know Germany not just from textbooks. I lived there, studied at German universities, worked in different fields, and experienced German culture from the inside.
